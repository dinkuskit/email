DinkusKit Store emails sends your shoppers the emails a store needs, from your store's own address.

- **Order confirmation** when an order is paid: the items with their options, subtotal, discount, shipping, sales tax, total, the ship-to address and a "View your order" button.
- **Shipping confirmation** when an order ships, with the carrier, the tracking number and a tracking link for USPS, UPS, FedEx and DHL. You can turn it off.
- **Abandoned cart email**, off until you turn it on: one email to a shopper who left a cart, with a link back to the same cart and a link to stop these emails. Send it to shoppers who ticked "Email me with news and offers", or to every customer.

Pick your logo from Media, a button colour and footer text, and email yourself a sample. Test orders go to you, not the shopper. The **Store emails** page lists recent emails with masked addresses.

Emails go through the sender you pick in **Settings > Email**. Cart emails are marketing, so they need an SMTP sender: Cloudflare's sender allows only transactional email.
