1. Use an EmDash site with Registry sandbox support, [DinkusKit Commerce](https://plugins.emdashcms.com/plugins/@dinkuskit.com/dinkus-commerce) 0.9.0 or later, and the DinkusKit store template, which wakes the store's courier once a minute.
2. Pick an email sender in **Settings > Email**. For abandoned cart emails, pick an SMTP sender.
3. Install DinkusKit Store emails and open its **Store emails** page. Order confirmations start right away. Choose whether to send shipping confirmations and abandoned cart emails, then add your logo and colours.
